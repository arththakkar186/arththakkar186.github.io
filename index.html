About me

Making secure access simple.

I'm Arth, an IT professional based in Toronto. I've spent the last five years as the onsite technical lead at large organizations, supporting executives, running site infrastructure and managing who can access what.

Identity and access management is where I do my best work. I use Microsoft Entra ID, Privileged Identity Management, access reviews and Conditional Access to give people what they need without leaving unnecessary admin rights in place, and I help keep SOX access audits clean. I've completed full SC-300 training and I'm preparing for the certification exam.

I also enjoy building. I use Claude and PowerShell to automate repetitive IT work, and I completed my computer science degree while working full-time.

How I work

Access should be temporary

Admin rights are requested, approved and time-limited, never permanent by default.

Security shouldn't slow people down

The best controls protect the business without getting in anyone's way.

Automate what repeats

If a task comes up twice, I script it and document it.
